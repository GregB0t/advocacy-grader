Read this before the numbers

This tool was built in a few days, on a stack of stated assumptions. I didn't have the time — or the inside knowledge of what buyers in this category actually optimize for — to be certain these are the metrics that matter most. The four categories and their weights are editorial judgment, not measurement: nobody has proven that these things cause employees to post, and I don't claim otherwise.

What isn't measured, on purpose: your employees' actual posting. I chose to strictly adhere to LinkedIn's Terms of Service, so no direct or indirect scraping of LinkedIn went into this report. The only LinkedIn signal here is whether your own pages carry a LinkedIn share link — that's your HTML, not theirs. This report reads only what your company has published on its own public site, which is also the part you can fix.

To prioritize speed, this particular report was loaded using cached data; it was generated from evidence originally collected on August 29, 2026 rather than live. Reports are cached for 30 days. Every observation below is dated and cited.

What you can hold me to: every number cites its public evidence, nothing unseen is guessed at, robots.txt is respected on every fetch, and when this tool can't read a site it says so instead of issuing a grade.

Opportunity

Your job listings are invisible without JavaScript

Your careers page (https://www.ssen-transmission.co.uk/about-us/careers) returns HTML, but the job listings are injected client-side — a plain reader sees a page with no roles on it. Machine readers that do not execute JavaScript (many crawlers and AI assistants) cannot see that you are hiring.

Evidence — careers page probes
  • https://www.ssen-transmission.co.uk/about-us/careers
  • https://www.ssen-transmission.co.uk/careers

The fix: Server-render at least the role titles and locations, or publish jobs in structured data (schema.org JobPosting).

Opportunity

No culture or life-at pages anywhere in your sitemap

Of 1,715 canonical URLs, zero classify as culture content — no life-at pages, employee stories, or team spotlights. This is the most common gap this tool finds, and the most directly advocacy-shaped one: it is the content employees share proudest and first.

Evidence: sitemap URL classification.

The fix: Start small: one employee-story post a month, indexed in the sitemap. The section can grow from there.
